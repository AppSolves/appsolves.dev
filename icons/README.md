# Google Play mark

`google-play.svg` preserves the four prism paths and viewBox from the official Google Play storefront header at https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.appsolves.tag_vault (retrieved 2026-10-08). The unchanged color original is retained at `assets/sources/google-play-original.svg`. `scripts/optimize-assets.mjs` replaces colored fills with a currentColor outline, retaining the recognizable prism and internal seams. The `mark` fragment is rendered locally at 16px beside the developer-page link, without a badge or runtime hotlink.
